ASPIRE — Three new directions
Variant A
Constellation Map
A geographic cluster of human-scale dots connected by fine lines — each node a real person, sized by activity. The core node anchors the whole system. Reads as "community coming into focus" rather than tech infrastructure. Holds up at favicon size because the core node is prominent. Low visual noise, high geographic intelligence signal.
Variant B
Fingerprint Network
Concentric arc-rings — the structure of a fingerprint — interrupted and bridged by node dots. Unique identity is the fingerprint; network is the bridging nodes. The most conceptually tight of the three: identity + community in one mark. The arc gaps draw the eye inward to the core, making it memorable at any size. No other AI company uses this form.
Variant C
Root System
A single verified source (the base node) branches upward into a growing network — like roots, dendrites, or a distribution network radiating from one point of truth. The two highlighted (blue/cyan) terminal nodes represent AI-activated growth branches. The metaphor is organic and grounded — growth that starts verified and spreads with purpose. Very distinctive silhouette; nothing else in civic tech uses this.
Choosing a direction: Variant A (Constellation) is the most immediately legible and sector-appropriate. Variant B (Fingerprint) is the most conceptually sharp and memorable — highest risk, highest reward. Variant C (Root System) has the strongest organic/human feel and most distinctive silhouette.
